### 🎙️ 5 Pro-Tips to Blow Judges Away

1. **Keep Your Resolution at 1080p (1920x1080)**: This ensures all Mercedes-Benz hairline borders, typography, and numbers are pin-sharp.
2. **Smooth Cursor Movement**: Move your mouse deliberately to the buttons. Don't shake or circle the mouse rapidly.
3. **Turn Off Background Music or Keep It Sub-audible**: The judges want to hear your voice clearly. If you use background music, choose a low-volume, minimal electronic ambient track at 8-10% volume.
4. **Use Built-in Windows Screen Recorder or Loom/OBS**:
   * Windows Shortcut: Press `Win + Alt + R` to start/stop recording immediately.
   * Loom: Great for adding a small circular webcam bubble of your face in the bottom-left corner (human connection adds trust!).
5. **Double Check Your Audio Mic**: Test a 5-second test clip before recording the full run to make sure your microphone is clear and loud.
